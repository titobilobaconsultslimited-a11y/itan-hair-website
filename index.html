Our Story

Born From
a Passion
for Hair

Itan Academy was founded with a singular mission — to elevate the standard of hair artistry in Nigeria and across Africa. What started as a small salon has grown into a movement, training hundreds of stylists and serving thousands of clients who trust us with their most prized possession.

Olutuase Olaitan — Founder & CEO
What We Stand For

Our Core Values

01
Integrity
We do what we say, and say what we mean. Honest dealings with every client, student, and partner are the foundation of everything we do.
02
Empathy
We listen first. Understanding each person's unique needs — whether in the salon chair or the classroom — shapes every experience we create.
03
Creativity
Hair is our canvas. We push boundaries, explore new techniques, and bring imaginative solutions to every style and every student we train.
04
Capacity Building
We invest in people. Through our academy programmes and mentorship, we equip individuals with the skills to build lasting, sustainable careers.
05
Spirituality
Grounded in purpose and guided by faith, we approach our work with gratitude, intentionality, and a deeper sense of meaning beyond the business.
06
Excellence
We hold every strand to the highest standard. Whether a simple trim or a complex colour, excellence is non-negotiable at Itan.
07
Empowerment
We believe transformation starts from within. We empower women, youth, and communities with the tools, confidence, and opportunity to thrive.
